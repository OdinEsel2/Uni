# Code of Conduct

This is a friendly, student-first project. By taking part you agree to:

- Be respectful, patient, and welcoming — many contributors are first-timers.
- Give helpful, constructive feedback. Critique ideas, not people.
- Keep contributions honest and accurate; cite sources.
- Protect privacy — don't share anyone's personal details without consent.

Harassment, discrimination, or spam isn't tolerated. To report a problem, open a
private issue or contact a maintainer. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions
that break these rules.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
