# Personal statements & applications

Real, **anonymised** examples and practical tips. Never post identifying details.

## Quick structure that works
1. **Hook** — a specific moment or reason you're drawn to the field.
2. **Evidence** — what you've actually done (projects, work, volunteering, reading).
3. **Fit** — why *this* course/uni, referencing specifics.
4. **Forward** — what you want to do with it.

## Tips
- Show, don't tell — concrete examples beat adjectives.
- One strong story > five shallow ones.
- Read it aloud; cut anything that sounds like a cliché.
- Get one other person to proofread.
